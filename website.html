The skin consultation

Have you ever actually seen what is happening underneath your skin?

This is the appointment everything starts with. The team images your skin, treats it, and puts your plan together while you are under the LED, so you can see and understand it before you leave.

01

We image

Photos of your skin on the Observ 520x skin analysis system, which has 10 observation modes.

02

We treat

A mini skin health facial, then LED.

03

We write

While you are under the LED, your plan comes together: your photos, what is going on in each one, and why each treatment and product is recommended.

04

We show you why

You go through your plan together, then choose your next step: your next treatment, the Skin Club, or your homecare.
