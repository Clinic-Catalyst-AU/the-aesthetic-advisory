Start with your concern

You do not need to know which machine. You need to know what your skin is doing, and why.

Nobody sits at home in Moranbah wondering whether they need IPL, Evo or DermaFrac. You notice something in the mirror. Choose what you are noticing and we will show you what the consultation looks at, and where the plan can go from there.

01

Pigmentation

Sun, hormones, the marks left behind. The Observ shows the pigment sitting under the surface, and the type decides the plan.
02

Dry and dehydrated

Tight, flaky, dull. Central Queensland air and a week on site take the water out of your skin. We put it back, and keep it there.
03

Acne and congestion

Breakouts that keep coming back, blocked pores, and the marks they leave. We treat the cause first, then the skin afterwards.
04

Pores and texture

Enlarged pores, rough texture that makeup sits in, scarring you stopped noticing but never stopped minding. Planned in stages.
05

Fine lines and ageing

The first lines, loss of firmness, skin that looks tired before you do. Usually several small things, put in the right order.
06

Skin after seven days on site

Dust, sun, air-conditioning, long days. If your skin feels different halfway through your swing, this is where we start.

Roster skin

Home for seven days? Here is when we would see you.

Most of our clients work a 7-on, 7-off roster. Your skin lives in two climates: site, and home. We plan around it, so treatment happens when your skin has time to settle, and homecare does the work while you are away.

Day 1 to 2 home

Reset

Your treatment, early in the swing. Skin has a full week to settle before you fly out, and you leave with the products packed for site.

Day 3 to 7 home

Recover

Homecare from your plan, LED if it is in your plan, and a Skin Club check-in by text if anything feels off. Nothing to remember.

7 days on site

Protect

The two or three products that actually go in the bag, SPF every morning, and a routine you can do in a donga bathroom in four minutes.

General guidance only. Your own timing is set at your consultation, for your skin and your treatment.
